3 Nights / 4 Days
Tour 01

Discover Sri Lanka – Short Island Escape

The perfect getaway for travelers with limited time who wish to witness the crown jewels of Sri Lanka's cultural heritage. From the coastal charm of Negombo to the awe-inspiring 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress, the golden caves of Dambulla, and the sacred Temple of the Tooth in royal Kandy.

Negombo Coastal Welcome
Sigiriya Rock Fortress Climb
Dambulla Cave Temple Complex
Kandy Temple of the Tooth
Traditional Kandyan Cultural Dance
Royal Botanical Gardens Peradeniya

Day 01 – Airport Arrival / Negombo

Warm Ayubowan welcome by Nirosh at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB). Transfer to your coastal hotel in Negombo. Relax by the beach and explore the famous fishing lagoon and catamarans.

Day 02 – Sigiriya / Dambulla

Journey inland to the UNESCO World Heritage Golden Cave Temple of Dambulla with ancient Buddha statues. In the afternoon, climb the legendary 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress of King Kashyapa for panoramic jungle vistas. Overnight in Sigiriya.

Day 03 – Kandy Cultural Experience

Drive through the fragrant spice groves of Matale to Kandy, the last royal capital of Sri Lanka. Visit the sacred Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), stroll around Kandy Lake, and enjoy an evening traditional Kandyan cultural dance. Overnight in Kandy.

Day 04 – Royal Botanical Gardens & Departure

Morning visit to the magnificent Peradeniya Royal Botanical Gardens with rare orchids and avenue of royal palms. Scenic transfer back to the international airport for your flight home.

5 Nights / 6 Days
Tour 02

Sri Lanka Highlights – Culture & Nature

Our most sought-after route connecting ancient royal citadels, sacred Buddhist shrines, rolling emerald tea estates in "Little England" Nuwara Eliya, and the dramatic mountain hiking trails of Ella with its world-famous Nine Arch Bridge.

Airport Meet & Negombo Lagoon
Sigiriya Rock Fortress & Dambulla Caves
Kandy Temple of the Tooth & Lake
Nuwara Eliya Working Tea Plantation
Demodara Nine Arch Bridge in Ella
Little Adam's Peak & Ravana Falls

Day 01 – Airport Arrival / Negombo

Arrive at CMB Airport and meet Nirosh. Check in to your beach resort in Negombo to refresh after your flight. Evening sunset stroll along Negombo beach.

Day 02 – Sigiriya / Dambulla

Travel into the Cultural Triangle. Discover Dambulla's rock temples dating back to the 1st century BC. Late afternoon ascent of Sigiriya Rock Fortress to view the lion paws and royal palace summit.

Day 03 – Kandy

Drive towards Kandy. Visit a traditional spice garden in Matale. In Kandy, explore the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, visit Peradeniya Botanical Gardens, and watch a vibrant evening cultural dance.

Day 04 – Nuwara Eliya (Tea Country)

Ascend into the misty highlands of Nuwara Eliya through cascading waterfalls and tea estates. Visit an authentic Ceylon tea factory to witness tea picking and processing, followed by tea tasting.

Day 05 – Ella

Board the iconic scenic train to Ella. Hike to the breathtaking Nine Arch Bridge in Demodara and climb Little Adam's Peak for panoramic views across Ella Gap. View dramatic Ravana Falls.

Day 06 – Departure

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast taking in the mountain views before our comfortable private transfer back to Colombo or CMB International Airport.

7 Nights / 8 Days
Tour 03 (Flagship)

Ultimate Sri Lanka Island Adventure

The complete Sri Lankan odyssey. Cultural wonders in Sigiriya and Kandy, misty Ceylon tea hills in Nuwara Eliya, the engineering marvel of Ella's Nine Arch Bridge, wild leopards and elephant herds in Yala National Park, and the colonial ramparts and golden beaches of Galle.

Sigiriya Rock & Dambulla Caves
Kandy Temple of the Tooth
Nuwara Eliya Highlands & Ceylon Tea
Ella Scenic Train & Nine Arch Bridge
Yala National Park 4x4 Jeep Safari
Galle Fort Heritage Walk & Lighthouse

Day 01 – Airport Arrival / Negombo

Arrival welcome by Nirosh. Check in to your Negombo seaside hotel. Unwind by the ocean and savor fresh tropical seafood.

Day 02 – Sigiriya / Dambulla

Travel inland to explore the magnificent Dambulla Cave Temple. In the afternoon, explore the UNESCO 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress with its famous lion paws and royal water gardens.

Day 03 – Kandy

Drive towards Kandy via Matale spice gardens. Visit the sacred Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, picturesque Kandy Lake, and attend an evening traditional cultural show.

Day 04 – Nuwara Eliya

Ascend through lush emerald mountain valleys. Tour a working colonial Ceylon tea factory, learn the art of tea plucking, and stroll through Gregory Lake and Nuwara Eliya's historic post office.

Day 05 – Ella

Experience the legendary hill-country train journey into Ella. Walk across the Demodara Nine Arch Bridge, hike Little Adam's Peak, and enjoy Ella's relaxed mountain café culture.

Day 06 – Yala Safari

Descend to the southern lowlands bordering Yala National Park. Embark on an exhilarating afternoon 4x4 private jeep safari to track leopards, wild elephants, sloth bears, and crocodiles.

Day 07 – Galle / Southern Coast

Travel along the sun-drenched southern coast past Mirissa and Weligama stilt fishermen. Explore the UNESCO World Heritage Galle Fort, its 18th-century ramparts, and iconic white lighthouse.

Day 08 – Departure

Transfer along the southern highway to Colombo or CMB International Airport, marking the conclusion of an unforgettable Traumurlaub Lanka island adventure.
