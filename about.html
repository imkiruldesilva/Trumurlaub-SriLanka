Passion for Sri Lanka

Sharing the True Essence of Our Pearl of the Indian Ocean

Traumurlaub Lanka was born out of a profound love for Sri Lanka and a dedication to showing the world its living culture, breathtaking landscapes, wildlife wonders, and warm, welcoming people.

We believe that travel is far more than visiting standard tourist attractions—it is about feeling the misty breeze at the top of Sigiriya Rock Fortress, hearing the gentle hum of early morning prayers at the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, catching the gaze of a wild leopard in Yala, and sitting beside the Indian Ocean at sunset with fresh coconut water.

Every itinerary we create is custom-designed around your desires, safety, comfort, and pace, supported by modern, air-conditioned private vehicles and certified English and German-speaking local guidance.

Meet Our Founder – Nirosh

With approximately 15 years in the Sri Lankan travel industry, Nirosh has earned a sterling reputation among European, British, and global travelers for integrity, deep cultural knowledge, and personalized customer care.

Nirosh began his journey with a simple vision: to treat every guest like an honored family member visiting Sri Lanka. Rather than rushing visitors through rigid cookie-cutter bus schedules, Nirosh tailors every itinerary to match individual interests—whether that is finding peaceful birdwatching trails, capturing the early morning sun over the Ella Nine Arch Bridge, or discovering authentic culinary experiences.

Here is why discerning international travelers rely on us for their dream Sri Lankan holiday.
